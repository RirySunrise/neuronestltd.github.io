Who we support
Is NeuroNest right for my child?

NeuroNest is designed for children aged 2 to 12 who need a thoughtful, lower-stimulation environment to play and grow. Children who may enjoy it include those who:

Are autistic or have ADHD
Have sensory processing differences
Prefer calmer, less crowded play environments
Need structured or supported play
Feel overwhelmed in typical play spaces
Are preparing for mainstream school
Benefit from OT or speech support
Simply need more space and patience to play

You do not need a diagnosis to visit. If your child needs a thoughtful, inclusive play environment, your family is welcome here. We also accommodate children with other additional needs, including Down Syndrome, cerebral palsy and other physical or developmental differences.
