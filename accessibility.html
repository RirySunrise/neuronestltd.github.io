Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 2026

NeuroNest Limited is a space built for neurodivergent children and their families. We want our website to be as usable as possible for everyone, including people with disabilities. This page explains what we have done, what we know still needs work, and how to reach us if you run into a problem.

What we have done

We have tried to build this website in a way that works for a wide range of people and devices. Specifically:

  • All pages use a clear heading structure so screen readers and keyboard users can navigate easily.
  • All images have descriptive alt text.
  • Colour contrast across the site is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards for text readability.
  • The site works on all screen sizes, including phones and tablets.
  • Links are clearly labelled and describe where they go.
  • Forms and interactive elements can be used with a keyboard alone.
  • The Social Story page uses plain language and a simple layout designed to be read with children who have communication differences.

Known limitations

We are a small organisation and we do not have a full accessibility audit in place yet. Some things we are aware of:

  • The Google Maps embed on our contact section is provided by Google and may not be fully accessible to all screen reader users. We include our full address in text on every page as an alternative.
  • Some of our gallery images are decorative and may have limited description for screen reader users.
  • Our site does not currently offer a text resize control or a high contrast toggle, though these can be set at the browser or operating system level.

What to do if you have a problem

If you find any part of this website difficult to use, please tell us. We will do our best to provide the information you need in a different format or to fix the problem.

Email: info@neuronestltd.com
WhatsApp: +254 768 199 515

Technical information

This website is built in standard HTML and CSS with no third-party frameworks. It is hosted on GitHub Pages at www.neuronestltd.com. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA where possible.

How we tested this site

We have reviewed the site manually and tested it across different browsers and screen sizes. We have not commissioned a formal third-party accessibility audit. We intend to do a more thorough review as the site grows.

Updates to this statement

We will update this page when we make significant changes to the site or when new accessibility issues are identified and addressed.
