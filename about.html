Who We Are

Our Story

Built by a family with lived experience. Shaped by professionals who understand what neurodivergent children need. Made for every family who has felt invisible in other spaces.

Mission

Play, therapy and community in one place

To empower neurodivergent children through inclusive play, education, therapy and community support, and to help families, schools, employers and communities build the spaces and systems that support them.

01
Vision

Free to be themselves, at every stage

An Africa where neurodivergent people are free to be themselves, supported by inclusive systems and spaces at every stage of life.

02
Long-term Goal

A sustainable model for lasting change

To build a sustainable model of inclusion, with schools, workplaces and public spaces that support neurodivergent people from childhood into adulthood, across Kenya and beyond.

03

NeuroNest is built primarily for neurodivergent children. Siblings and neurotypical friends are genuinely welcome too. We believe children grow up kinder and more understanding when they play side by side.

Who we are and why we exist

NeuroNest was built by a family with lived experience of raising a neurodivergent child in Nairobi. Finding a play space that genuinely worked for their child, one designed for them rather than merely tolerant of them, proved almost impossible.

The playground should be the simplest thing. But too much noise, too many distractions, people staring. Their child could not play or integrate the way other children did. After being asked to leave a play area within thirty minutes, and watching their child retreat to the quietest corner of another space just to cope, the family made a decision: build what does not exist.

For a full year, the founding family consulted occupational therapists, speech therapists, behavioural specialists, and early childhood development experts. NeuroNest opened in January 2026, a space built from lived experience rather than good intentions.

"
We want every parent who walks through that door to feel like they have arrived. Like someone finally knows what they have been through. And like their child is safe here, truly safe.
The NeuroNest Founding Family

The play area is just the beginning

We are building a full ecosystem of support for neurodivergent children and their families, including access for families who cannot afford market rates.

When typical children grow up playing alongside neurodivergent children, understanding grows and fear does not. Inclusion shapes the adults those children become and the society we all share. That work starts here.
